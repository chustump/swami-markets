# Swamisays.ai — Market Strategy

> **One-liner:** The AI oracle for prediction markets. Find edge across Polymarket and Kalshi, ask any forward-looking question, and bet smarter.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Swamisays.ai turns prediction markets into a consumer product. Two assets are
combined into a single experience:

1. **Live cross-platform data** — Polymarket × Kalshi prices, volume, and
   spreads on the same markets, side-by-side.
2. **The Swami** — an AI-native oracle that translates news, base rates, and
   sentiment into a probability, a side (YES / NO / LEAN), and a confidence.

The wedge is **edge discovery**: retail prediction-market users today juggle
two apps, X feeds, and group chats to find mispriced markets. Swamisays.ai
collapses that loop into one dashboard with a daily Top 5 and an Ask interface.

The strategy: launch with a sharp free product that produces shareable daily
content, convert engaged users into a paid Pro tier for real-time edges, and
build a public track record that compounds trust.

## 3. Target Customer

### Primary — "The Edge-Seeker" (60% of revenue target)
- Trades $500–$10K bankroll on Polymarket and/or Kalshi
- Active on X, follows @PolymarketIntel, @KalshiOddsBot, @Aaron_Slodov

- Willingness to pay: **$20–$50/mo** for real-time edge

- Politics-, crypto-, or sports-obsessed; opinionated; not a power trader
- Comes for "what does the market think about X?" — stays for daily picks
- Converts at lower rate but drives viral distribution
- Willingness to pay: **$5–$15/mo** or ad-supported free

### Tertiary — "The Sharp" (10%, but anchors credibility)
- Quant-ish, $25K+ bankroll, would use an API
- Pain: needs structured spread data, backtests, alerts
- Willingness to pay: **$99–$299/mo** for API + watchlist tier

## 4. Positioning

> **"The Oracle for Prediction Markets."**

Three pillars, every surface reinforces them:

1. **Cross-platform by default.** Every market shown is Polymarket × Kalshi.
2. **Probability-first.** Swami publishes a probability and side, not a hot take.
3. **Receipts.** Track record is public and updated automatically.

**What we are not:** a sportsbook, a hot-takes newsletter, an aggregator with
no opinion. The opinion — calibrated, evidenced, and scored — is the product.

## 5. Product Strategy & Tiers

### Free
- Daily **Top 5 Picks** (delayed 30 min)
- **H2H view** of ~25 trending markets
- **Ask Swami** — 3 questions/day, basic answer
- Public track record / Brier score

### Pro — $19/mo (or $149/yr)
- **Real-time** Top 5 + edge alerts (push + email)
- Unlimited Ask Swami with structured output (probability, reasoning, comparable base rates)
- Full H2H universe (~500 markets) with spread sorting
- Custom watchlists and price-cross alerts
- No ads, support for portfolio import (read-only)

### Sharps — $99/mo (or $899/yr)
- Everything in Pro
- **API access** to Swami probabilities and spreads
- **Backtester** — replay any pick on historical liquidity
- Calibration export (CSV/Parquet), webhook firehose
- Discord channel with weekly call from operators

### Why three tiers (not two)
- Free has to be good enough to share — that is the growth engine.
- Pro is the volume tier; price-anchored against Action Network ($8/mo) and
  premium Substacks ($10–$15/mo), positioned slightly above because it is a
  tool, not content.
- Sharps unlocks a willingness-to-pay tail we would otherwise leave on the
  table, and the public existence of an API tier signals "real product."

## 6. Go-to-Market Plan

### Phase 1 — Stealth & Seed (Weeks 1–4)
Goal: 1,000 waitlist signups, locked-in launch creative.
- Ship the existing waitlist + Top 5 + Ask Swami flows publicly behind a
  "preview" banner.
- Hand-pick **20 design-partner traders** from X replies and Polymarket
  Discord. Free Pro for life in exchange for weekly feedback.
- Daily Swami Pick posted to X at 9:00 ET. Same artwork, same format, every
  day. Build pattern recognition.
- SEO foundation: ship `/compare/polymarket-vs-kalshi`,
  `/markets/[slug]`, and a `/track-record` page indexed from day one.

### Phase 2 — Public Launch (Weeks 5–12)
Goal: 10,000 waitlist → users, 500 paid Pro, $10K MRR.
- **Launch day:** Product Hunt, Hacker News (Show HN), simultaneous X thread
  with the running track record from Phase 1.
- **Creator program:** seed 10 prediction-market / political-betting creators
  with free Sharps + shareable embed widget for their Substacks.
- **Daily content loop:**
  - Morning Pick (X + email)
  - Afternoon H2H spread of the day
  - Evening recap of yesterday's calls
- **Paid pilot:** $5K/mo experimenting on X promoted posts targeted at
  followers of Polymarket, Kalshi, Manifold, @PredictIt accounts.

### Phase 3 — Compound (Months 4–6)
Goal: 50K users, 2K paid, $50K MRR.
- **Referral:** refer 3, get 1 month Pro; refer 10, get 1 year.
- **Affiliate partnerships** with Polymarket and Kalshi — they pay CAC, we
  deliver activated users.
- **API tier launch** to Sharps; recruit 1–2 quant funds as beta users for
  case studies.
- **Vertical expansion** to sports (Kalshi sports contracts) and crypto
  (FTX-style perp prediction markets if regulation permits).

## 8. Growth Loops

Three loops that should each compound, separately:

1. **Daily Pick → Share → Sign-up.** Every pick has a one-tap share-card. The
   card carries the brand, the call, and a track-record stat. Optimize the
   card before the funnel.
2. **Track Record → Trust → Conversion.** Brier score, hit-rate, ROI on a
   $100/pick portfolio, all public, all auto-updated. Calibration credibility
   is the moat; do not fake it, do not hide losses.
3. **Referral → Free Pro → Activation.** A Pro user who refers 3 friends has
   meaningfully higher LTV; bake the referral CTA into the loss-recap email
   (counterintuitive, but losses are when users want validation).

## 14. What This Strategy Bets Against

Calling out the contrarian bets, so we can revisit them honestly in 90 days:

1. **Brand matters in tools.** Most B2C tools converge on minimalism. We are
   betting that "The Swami" character creates memorability and social-share
   lift that a generic dashboard cannot.
2. **Calibration sells.** Most retail trading content sells confidence. We
   are betting a public, sometimes-ugly Brier score will out-compound hype.
3. **Cross-platform is the wedge.** Most users will stay on one platform. We
   are betting that the highest-LTV users are exactly the ones who won't, and
   they are who we serve.

If any of these are wrong, the plan above needs to change, not just the
tactics underneath it.
