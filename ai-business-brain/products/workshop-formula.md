# Workshop formula (Veritasium structure)

Total runtime: 90–120 minutes. Leave 15 minutes for application + offer.

## Cold open (3 min)
A specific scene. Not “AI is changing everything.”

## Misconception (10–12 min)
State the belief in their words.
Examples from the video’s world:
- “I need a new content idea every day.”
- “I need to be the best in my market.”
- “ChatGPT Projects is already a system.”
- “I should run ads once I have a course.”

Make them feel seen. If they don’t nod here, the rest fails.

## Why the misconception exists (10 min)
Agree with the logic. Show the incentives (platforms, gurus, tools, fear).
This is respect, not a dunk.

## The truth (20–25 min)
One model. Teach it with:
- a diagram they can redraw
- one real example from your work
- the failure mode

## Application (20–25 min)
They do the thing live.
Not “go build your whole company.”
One artifact: a context file, a series premise, an offer sentence, a 7-day calendar.

## Proof, scoped (8 min)
Only numbers and stories you can defend.
Include the ugly parts (what did not work).

## Offer (10 min)
- What the next 30 days look like with you
- Price
- Who should not buy
- What happens after they pay
- FAQ you actually get

## Close (5 min)
Restate the new belief in one line.
Tell them the first 15-minute action for tomorrow morning.

## Design rules
- Teach fewer things than you know.
- One new vocabulary word max.
- Every story must earn a principle.
- If you need 40 slides, you do not have a workshop yet.
