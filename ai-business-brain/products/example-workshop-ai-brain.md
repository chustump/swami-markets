Only use this example if you genuinely help operators install AI systems.
If that is not you, treat this as a *shape*, then run `prompts/02-productize.md` on your own knowledge.

## Working title
**Stop prompting. Install a business brain.**
A 2-hour live workshop to turn your scattered ChatGPT chats into a folder that actually runs work.

## Promise
By the end you will have Layer 1 of your brain on disk (identity, voice, customer, offer, knowledge) and a first skill you can run tomorrow.

## Misconception
“I already use AI. I just need better prompts.”

## Truth
Prompts are disposable. Context files compound. The business that wins is the one whose AI has memory, taste, and jobs — not a longer chat history.

## Application
Build the five Layer 1 files live. Ingest one transcript. Generate one workshop outline *or* one week of content from that brain.
