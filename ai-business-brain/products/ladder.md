# Product ladder (copy this shape, not Stanley’s prices)

Stanley’s shape, generalized:

```
Attention (organic + ads)
        ↓
Low-ticket live workshop     $97–$197     belief change + list
        ↓
Mid-ticket implementation    $500–$2k     they do it with a template + you
        ↓
High-ticket delivery         $5k–$20k+    you do the thing your agency/practice already does
```

## Why this order
- Courses have terrible completion. A live room creates a shared moment and a next-step conversation.
- Low-ticket lets you run ads without hopping on every call.
- High-ticket still needs trust. The workshop is the filter, not the whole business.

## Your first product test (14 days)
1. One misconception you can kill with a story + a framework you already use.
2. A registration page with a real date.
3. 20–50 people from your existing network before any ad spend.
4. Record it. That becomes the replay asset.
5. Only then buy attention.

## Naming
Name the transformation, not the tool.
Bad: “Claude Code Masterclass”
Better: “Stop chatting with AI. Install a business brain in one afternoon.”
