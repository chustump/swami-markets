# Skill: Draft the sales stack

When the human is ready to sell the low-ticket.

## Produce
1. Offer name, price, promise
2. Stripe product description (plain)
3. Thank-you page copy
4. Klaviyo / email tags and 7-email post-purchase sequence
5. Meta campaign structure:
   - Campaign objective: sales
   - 1–3 ad sets max
   - Creative angles from research
   - Starting budget suggestion: small and observable (e.g. $20–$50/day) until signal, not $500/day on day 1 unless they have cash and a proven page
6. Tracking checklist (pixel, events, refund policy)
7. 14-day experiment plan: what “kill / iterate / scale” looks like

## Honesty rule
Do not copy Stanley’s $90k/month spend as a default. Match `context/06-constraints.md`.
Scale only after creative + page convert.
