# Skill: Market research (Stanley method)

## Goal
Validate or kill an offer in hours using language customers already use.

## Inputs
- Hypothesis from `context/03-customer.md` and `context/04-offer.md`
- Niche + platform list

## Process
1. Restate the hypothesis in one sentence.
2. Search publicly discussed language (Reddit, forums, YouTube comments, review sites, social threads):
   - “I don’t have time to…”
   - “I tried X and…”
   - “Does anyone else struggle with…”
   - pricing complaints
   - tool complaints (ChatGPT, agencies, courses)
3. Cluster into 5 pains, 5 desired outcomes, 5 objections.
4. Quote 15 raw lines. Do not paraphrase first.
5. Score the hypothesis:
   - Demand evidence: high / medium / low
   - Crowdedness
   - Whether our proof in `01-identity.md` is enough
6. Recommend: kill / tighten / ship a workshop
7. Output a positioning line that uses *their* words, not ours.

## Output file
`/outputs/research-[slug].md`
