# Skill: Build a live workshop

Follow `/products/workshop-formula.md`.

## Deliverables
Write all of these into `/outputs/workshop-[slug]/`:
1. `title-and-promise.md` — 10 title options + 1 chosen
2. `outline.md` — timed 2-hour rundown
3. `slides-or-talk-track.md` — speaker notes, not slide essays
4. `workbook.md` — 1-page application
5. `registration-page.md` — sales page copy
6. `emails.md` — 5 emails (announce, remind, replay, upsell)
7. `ads.md` — 8 Meta primary texts + 8 headlines + 5 hooks for video
8. `offer-stack.md` — what they get + bump + next step

## Constraints
- One misconception. One transformation.
- No module dump.
- Teach thinking, then one application.
- Soft CTA at 70% of the way through, hard CTA at the end.
- No income claims.
