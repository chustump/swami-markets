How it works

Three steps, then it runs every morning without you.

Set it up once on a 20-minute call. After that the edge list lands before the open.

  1. STEP 01

    Tell Swami what you trade

    Rates, elections, weather, crypto, sports — pick the categories and the minimum edge worth waking up for. Everything outside your list stays out of your inbox.

  2. STEP 02

    Swami scores the whole board

    Overnight it re-forecasts every open contract from base rates, news flow and order-book behaviour, then compares its number to the last traded price.

  3. STEP 03

    You get the gaps at 7:00am

    One email, ranked by edge in cents, each line showing Swami’s probability, the market price, and the reasoning in two sentences. Buy, fade, or skip.
